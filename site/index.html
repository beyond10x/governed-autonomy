The idea

Agents choose how to do the work. Your organization defines what must be true before the work counts.

Most agent systems are built around execution: an intent goes in, an agent calls tools, a result comes out. What they leave out is the part an organization actually cares about: who allowed this, what had to be established, and whether it is really done. Beyond10x is that missing layer.

How it works

A governed case around every piece of work

Every intent becomes a case. The case knows what must happen, what may happen and what is actually true right now. Agents work inside it. Real-world observations come back as evidence, and the case is re-evaluated until a legitimate outcome has been earned.

Protocol

What must happen?

The obligations your way of working imposes: steps, checks and conditions that define the work.

Authority

What may happen?

Who may authorize what. Which actions agents take freely, which need approval, which are forbidden.

Knowledge

What is actually true?

What has been established, by what evidence, about which revision. And what is still unknown.

Principles

What we hold the system to

Completion is earned

Most automation treats "the process finished" as success. We do not.

  • A model run completing is not completion.
  • A workflow finishing is not completion.
  • A pull request merging is not completion.
  • A successful deployment is not necessarily completion.

Work is complete when the conditions its protocol defines are satisfied. That can mean evidence, observation over time, independent verification, approval or explicit acceptance.

Autonomy inside boundaries

We do not script every step. That would reduce autonomy to workflow automation. Agents get a governed frontier instead: what is known, what is required, what is allowed, what needs authority, and what would move the case forward. Inside it, they are free.

Intent survives the whole journey

What was asked for, how it was interpreted, what work was done, what authority was exercised, what reached production, and whether the original intent actually became true. One line, from request to outcome.

Your way of working becomes executable

Policies, runbooks, checklists, approval chains and review habits usually sit in documents that different people read differently. Beyond10x turns them into protocols that govern every autonomous worker the same way.

Deterministic where it matters

Agents are probabilistic: they reason, investigate and adapt. What is permitted, what is required, which evidence is admissible and whether completion was earned are decided deterministically. Final authority never rests on a model's judgment alone.

Where this goes

An operating layer for autonomous organizations

Organizations will delegate more and more consequential work to autonomous systems. For that to work, those systems have to take part in how the organization operates: how work is conducted, what authority exists, what evidence is trusted, which risks need escalation and which outcomes are acceptable. We are building the layer that makes that possible.
